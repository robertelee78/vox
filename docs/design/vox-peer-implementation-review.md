The nested-worker case has a concrete legal schedule: 32 Entry requests each retain two 128-KiB metadata workers, occupying all 64 foreground positions with 8 MiB of metadata, then all ask for a third worker needed to finish. Inventory can similarly retain 64 input workers at about 1.25 MiB each. These are structural source arguments, not measured runtime reproductions. Reusing the actual worker removes the recursive permit edge while preserving the complete simultaneous byte envelope. Raising a cap merely moves the cycle.

Syncthing also uses retry timers and optional durability choices; Iroh includes grace/backoff timers and lossy transport semantics. These are observed implementation choices, not recommendations to copy constants, weaken durable receipt guarantees or replace causal progress with a deadline. QUIC application streams do not each perform a new TLS handshake. Bounded batching remains a hypothesis requiring its own evidence and authority/credit/fairness review.

## Current unresolved boundaries

The U6 apparatus exposed a distinction the review must retain: an **outer Archive operation can complete a semantic Body under an actual Control/LargeControl job after a legitimate yield**. `runtime.rs::lane` selects Archive for History Entry/Page wire work, while local phases select Control; `Job::record_job` can promote reconstruction to LargeControl. The old uncommitted pressure hook and its mutant both filtered the worker lane as Archive. An arm-only run where the chosen row became readable therefore does not establish which producer rendered it and does not establish real completion pressure. An absent readable row also does not prove its body is absent: stored-body key/consent backfill is another valid producer (`channel.rs::drain_backfill_into`). Corrected semantic request binding/actual-lane observation remains apparatus work, not a product fix or a per-lane proof result. No pressure green or mutant bite is asserted here.

A separate current trace at product `3abd0e1dacea330c540b08ddd0bee18819921ac7` shows a failed explicit Cancel result being overwritten by observational Status: Cancel creates Preparing, its worker errors, acceptance installs Unavailable with the pending missing request, and Status starts a new preparation rather than returning that explicit failure. Later Status reloads Paused and hides the Cancel failure (`actor/history_demand.rs::begin_history_demand_with_job/accept_history_demand`). This state-ownership defect and the underlying Cancel error classification remain unresolved RCA, not implemented fixes. Cancelled cannot be claimed before actual durable cancellation and required cleanup.

General Archive donor recovery and the independent first-Paused activation mismatch also remain unresolved. A two-member Bob-only fixture avoids an unrelated donor ambiguity; it does not fix donor selection. The optional diagnostic, existing user-journey proofs, and their negative controls remain distinct from source review. Every red keeps PRODUCT versus APPARATUS attribution; this note adds no test, CI gate, internal counter, deadline, or permission to declare release readiness.
