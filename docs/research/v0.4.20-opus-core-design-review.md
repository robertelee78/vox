## Findings

**1. Returning-author recovery depends on a membership standing the design fences off (P1, confidence 0.85)**
`docs/adr/ADR-023-room-lifecycle.md:262-272` (also ADR-037 lines 170-178)
- **Design:** RL-8.5 and the ADR-037 amendment hold the return Presence until own-history recovery and key recovery finish, and say permitted reads stay live meanwhile.
- **Source:** peers stop counting a member once its feed ends in the leave Presence (`channel.rs:8658-8681`, `is_member = is_author && !has_left`).
- **Donor gates need membership:** recovery pages are served only to members (`own_history.rs:597,651`). Completing recovery requires the node itself to be a member (`own_history.rs:714`). Key re-delivery skips a left member (`channel.rs:4696,4710`). The only exception is a departed author fetching its own head receipt (`history_stream/source.rs:497-503`).
- **Readmission is narrow:** only the member that answered the join readmits the returner, and only in memory (`channel.rs:1421-1422,8710-8721`; `pending_roster.rs:503`). Other peers, or that member after a restart, keep treating the returner as gone until its own "I'm back" Presence arrives. Today that Presence is appended during recovery (`pending_recovery.rs:514-517`).
- **Consequence:** recovery and "reads stay live" cannot be met with RL-8.1b unchanged.
- **Fix:** RL-8.5 must name the authenticated standing peers honour before the return Presence. One option is a durable admission notice newer than the leave, which already exists, is kept and travels through boards; peers would honour it for recovery pages, own-generation release and key delivery. Otherwise, drop "reads remain live" and the RL-8.8 pending-read row. Either way, amend RL-8.1b's "ordinary join" wording.

**2. Authors with no marker still sign a replacement prefix (P1, confidence 0.8)**
`docs/adr/ADR-023-room-lifecycle.md:266-272`
- RL-8.5 lets an author with no prior continuity settle locally as a fresh author.
- Every node that left under 0.4.19 or earlier has no marker. On rejoin it is indistinguishable from a fresh author and goes through `channel.rs:2970-3127`, signing new seq 1 and 2 entries exactly as the diagnostic run showed.
- That breaks FX-54's unqualified "Normal leave/rejoin MUST NOT sign a replacement author prefix".
- **Fix:** either scope FX-54 and RL-8.5 to leaves made by a marker-writing release and state the legacy outcome in the release notes, or require the joiner to check the admitting peer for its own self-signed head before local settlement. That head cannot be forged; its absence only means "unknown".

**3. RL-8.6's bare-tombstone removal would weaken genuine room ends (P1, confidence 0.85)**
`docs/adr/ADR-023-room-lifecycle.md:281-284`
- The clause says a bare local withdrawn-room marker is not authoritative and may be cleared once admission allows reconstruction.
- A genuine end also writes a bare tombstone: every member holding the room calls `forget_room_on_board` → `withdraw_room(cid, None)` once its log shows the end (`actor.rs:20604,20643-20644`; `network.rs:2651-2657`; `store.rs:333-334`, "None for a node's own forget").
- An idle end has no signed board withdrawal at all on non-creator nodes (`actor.rs:20610-20616`).
- The board store is in memory only and rebuilt empty at every daemon start (`network.rs:612-619`). Wrongly created tombstones therefore do not survive the upgrade restart. The clause has nothing legitimate to clean up and would demote real ends.
- **Fix:**
  - Delete the sentence starting "A bare local withdrawn-room marker without authenticated end evidence…".
  - Rely on classifying the producers: only ended-here rooms and received signed Room withdrawals use the terminal operation.
  - Correct RCA §9 (line 495, "permanently rejects", "never reverses") to say the tombstone lasts until the daemon restarts. [INFERENCE] Startup journal recovery may re-run the retraction while its cleanup journal remains.

**4. The post-admission "durable obligation" has no workable mechanism (P1, confidence 0.8)**
`docs/adr/ADR-023-room-lifecycle.md:295-299`
- **The joiner cannot tell whether admission committed.** The responder commits in `admit_before_accepting` before sending `Accepted` (`joinstream.rs:1259-1291`). If `Accepted` is lost after `AdmissionPrepared` (`joinstream.rs:1053-1076`), the joiner has no witness either way.
- **There is no resume path.** The witness travels only in `Accepted`. The passphrase is not kept after a failed attempt (`actor.rs:3525,10643`). The room's at-rest key is sealed under a key derived from the passphrase (`atrest/sek.rs:10-18`; `actor.rs:4520-4529`).
- **Today's abort undoes the admission.** It promotes the Member withdrawal journalled earlier at the proposal's epoch and time (`actor.rs:3663-3697`; `metadata_save.rs:3955-4012`), which voids the committed notice. "Retry the same owned admission" contradicts that.
- **Fix:** define three cases:
  - The obligation starts only when a verified `Accepted` witness arrives. Persist the witness, proposal and genesis reference in the existing join journal before the name and construction checks.
  - A lost `Accepted` means commit-uncertain. Promote the joiner's own signed Member withdrawal and report that the host may have admitted this node. Retry is a fresh PAKE that must be strictly newer (RL-8.6).
  - After a verified `Accepted`, suppress that withdrawal while resuming. State whether resuming needs the passphrase re-entered or the existing held-room key storage. The name reservation must persist with the obligation, which reconciles the RCA's "release on crash-recovery" wording.

**5. A removed member can keep posting (P1, confidence 0.75)**
`docs/adr/ADR-007-membership-consent-and-admin-governance.md:226-249`
- G-31 fences queued sends, key delivery, Session read/Drive and services, but says nothing about the target's own future entries.
- Under G-17, an admitted author's entries still pass the log check. Under G-24, the target alone releases its own sender key. A later entry clears its left state (`channel.rs:8690-8702`, per the scout reading).
- So a removed node can go on posting messages that remaining members who trust it can read. G-32 has no row for this.
- **Fix:** in G-31, once a removal is applied, members do not accept, forward or display the target's entries outside the removal's causal past, and do not accept its sender keys for the room. In G-32, add a consumer row (removed node posts; peers' native views don't show it) with a mutant that accepts the post and fails.

**6. G-29 and G-31 contradict each other on missing dependencies (P2, confidence 0.75)**
`docs/adr/ADR-007-membership-consent-and-admin-governance.md:226-230`
- G-29 says a removal with missing ancestry must not take effect. G-31 says "unresolved required governance dependencies" win over queued sends.
- The evaluator has no pending state today: absent predecessors are dropped (`evaluator.rs:1070-1080`) and unverifiable entries skipped (`evaluator.rs:262-270`).
- As written, any member could stall egress indefinitely with a fact pointing at ancestry nobody has.
- **Fix:** only validated removals fence egress. Unvalidated facts stay inert and are re-evaluated when their ancestry arrives. Remove "or unresolved required governance dependencies".

**7. The marker's scope and contents are not pinned down (P2, confidence 0.7)**
`docs/adr/ADR-023-room-lifecycle.md:253-261`
- RL-8.2's End deletes the room "as RL-8.1 does", so the Leave-only marker exception the operator approved would silently extend to ended rooms and be kept forever.
- "Sufficient cryptographic append/recovery frontier" leaves the contents open.
- **Fix:** state that End erases the marker. Define the marker as the last own signed head (the existing recovery head type) plus the highest sender chain/generation, with the frontier derived during verified recovery.

**8. Terminal-across-restart quietly adds durability the product doesn't have (P2, confidence 0.65)**
`docs/adr/ADR-012-nat-traversal-and-reachability.md:234-238`
- N-27b and RCA §1 acceptance item 4 require ends to stay terminal "across recovery/restart".
- Board tombstones are in memory only (`network.rs:612-619`). After the room is deleted locally, the only durable evidence is the `pending-board-withdrawals` ledger (`startup_save.rs:64,541`; `actor.rs:11181`), and its lifetime is unconfirmed.
- **Fix:** name that ledger, or the log, as the durable source; or drop "restart" from the claim.

**9. FX-61's acceptance can't go red as specified (P2, confidence 0.65)**
`docs/adr/ADR-038-daemons-trust-daemons-and-sessions-are-presence.md:1451-1461`
- The controlled overlap run was green: fresh posts crossed both ways. Post/read observations alone cannot produce the scoped product failure that ADR-018's mutant rule requires.
- **Fix:** stage the interrupted work as the only pending obligation, for example a selected-then-superseded key package for a newly admitted member with no later command, and observe that member's read. Otherwise record FX-61 as Blocked pending a reproduction or a reported spike.

**10. Existing forked identities have no stated outcome (P2, confidence 0.6)**
`docs/adr/ADR-023-room-lifecycle.md:269-272`
- RL-8.5 keeps already-conflicting history fail-closed, but doesn't say what that identity (Vince's OMP node) can do in that room: whether it can read, what it may write, and which operator remedy applies (new identity per K-8, or the G-28 removal).
- **Fix:** state that outcome and the honest health status.

**11. FX-53 overstates the cause (P3, confidence 0.7)**
`docs/adr/ADR-038-daemons-trust-daemons-and-sessions-are-presence.md:1413-1414`
- "The confirmed cause" holds for the source and the isolated reproduction. The RCA itself says the attribution to Vince's incident is inferred; his board was not inspected.
- **Fix:** qualify the sentence accordingly.

**12. Two smaller gaps in the removal fact (P3, confidence 0.5)**
`docs/adr/ADR-007-membership-consent-and-admin-governance.md:208-215`
- **Mixed versions:** G-29 lacks RL-8.7's handling for older versions. Older nodes reject the new struct tag as unknown (`wire.rs:178`; `error.rs:25`). [INFERENCE] What an old member then does with the admin's feed was not traced.
- **Capability:** G-29 doesn't name the capability the evaluator checks. Delegated admin certificates carry only `policy` (`evaluator.rs:689-697`).

## Verdict
- **Overall correctness:** incorrect.
- **Summary:** findings 1–5 are unresolved P1 design blockers: recovery and readmission ordering, legacy returners, genuine-end tombstones, the post-admission commit-uncertainty mechanism, and post-removal writes. Findings 6–12 are smaller fixes. FX-53, FX-54 and FX-62 should stay Designing/Blocked until the clauses above are revised and reviewed again.
- **Confidence:** 0.75.
